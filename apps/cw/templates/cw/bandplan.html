Band Chart

Where you may key up, by license class — and where the CW operators actually are. US amateur allocations, IARU Region 2. The bars show the Extra-class allocation; the tick marks show where General and Technician privileges begin.

Two different kinds of line on this page. Subband edges come from FCC Part 97 and are law. Calling frequencies are voluntary band-plan convention — nobody is breaking a rule by working 7.031. This is a bench reference: before you transmit, check the ARRL band chart and the current FCC rules, which are the authority.
