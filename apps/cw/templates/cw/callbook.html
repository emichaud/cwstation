{# ── ACCOUNT ── #}

Account — XML lookups

Your QRZ login powers callsign lookups (needs a QRZ XML subscription). To change the password later, type the new one here and Save & test — it's write-only, encrypted at rest, and never shown again.

{# ── QRZ LOGBOOK SYNC ── #}

QRZ logbook — import / export

QRZ hosts its own logbook at logbook.qrz.com, with a separate per-logbook API key (on QRZ: My Logbook → Settings → API) — not your account password. With the key saved: Import pulls your entire QRZ log in here (duplicates skipped, safe to repeat — the way to feel at home on day one), and Export pushes contacts logged here up to QRZ, marking them so nothing double-sends.

{# ── HOW IT FITS ── #}

How the pieces fit

  • No account needed: every callsign in the app links to its QRZ page (qrz↗).
  • XML login: quick-logs and the QSO form auto-fill operator name, QTH, grid, and country.
  • Logbook key: your QRZ log and this log stay in sync — import once to bring your history home, export whenever you've logged new contacts.
  • ADIF is the common tongue: the same UTC-clean records go to QRZ, eQSL, or any file you ↓ export.
