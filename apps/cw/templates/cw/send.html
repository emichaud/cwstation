Timing is standard PARIS (dit = 1200 ms ÷ WPM) with raised-cosine keying — no clicks. The generated WAV can drive a rig's audio input or serve as practice material.

Message keys

Your keyer memories — like a contest keyer's F-keys. Type /cq in the composer (or click a keycap) and the message drops in with its {tags} expanded. Edit here; saves on blur.

Custom tags

Reusable values you can drop into any message. Define a tag once — say rig = KW4420 — then write {rig} in a message key or straight in the composer, and it expands to KW4420 when you insert a key and again when you press Key it. Great for your rig, antenna, name, QTH, or power.

{mycall}, {call}, and {rst} are filled by the station, so you can't reuse those names.
